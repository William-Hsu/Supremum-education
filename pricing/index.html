FAQ

Common questions

Do you complete assignments or assessments for students?+

No — my role is to build understanding and skills, not to complete work on a student's behalf. Everything we work through in sessions is designed to help you get there yourself.

How do I book the free trial?+

Send an email to hello@supremum.education with your name, the subject you need help with, and a few times that work. I'll confirm within 24 hours.

Where do sessions take place?+

All sessions are conducted online via Google Meet or Zoom. Just let me know which you prefer when you reach out.

Do I need to commit to a set number of sessions?+

No. There are no packages or minimum commitments. You book sessions as you need them and stop whenever you like.

How do I pay?+

Payment is accepted via e-transfer after each session. Details will be provided when you book.

Can you help with specific assignments or exams?+

Absolutely. Sessions can focus on exam prep, problem sets, or building foundations — whatever is most useful at that point in your term.

What if the free trial isn't a good fit?+

No problem and no hard feelings. The trial exists precisely so you can make that call without any financial commitment.
